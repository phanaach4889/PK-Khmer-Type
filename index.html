    Terms & Conditions

    PK Khmer Type — demo account system

    This is a demo account system. No real personal data should be entered — accounts exist only in this browser.

    01Where your data lives

    Everything you enter — username, email, and a hashed password — is saved to this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing leaves this device.

    02Password handling

    Passwords are never stored as plain text. Each one is combined with a random salt and hashed with SHA-256 before it touches storage. This is still not real-world security — it simply avoids the most obvious mistake.

    03Staying signed in

    Once you sign in, your session is kept on this device until you explicitly sign out — including after refreshing the page or navigating back and forward.

    04Clearing your data

    Clearing your browser's site data, using a different browser, or switching devices will remove your demo account. There is no recovery — this is a local-only trainer, not a hosted service.
