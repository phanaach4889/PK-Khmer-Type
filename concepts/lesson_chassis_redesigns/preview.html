Aerospace Modular Rack

Splits target key instruction, manuscript tape, and telemetry stats into distinct, non-competing visual chambers to eliminate cognitive friction.

  • ▸ Dedicated Biomechanics Pod with finger joint guidance
  • ▸ Continuous horizontal tape cursor eliminates vertical head bobbing
  • ▸ Real-time WPM & Accuracy telemetry clustered on the right flank

Why 44% Opacity is the Golden Ratio

Prior 85% opacity blocked 35% of the secondary legends on mechanical keys. At 44%, hand outlines remain 100% visible while all Khmer sub-glyphs shine through clearly.

  • ▸ High contrast legibility of Shift, Coeng, and subscript glyphs
  • ▸ Zero occlusion when typing fast combos or complex clusters
  • ▸ Subtle glowing pulse on the active reaching finger

Best Suited For

Intermediate to power users, competitive typists, and students focusing on rapid muscle memory acquisition and data-driven improvement.

  • ▸ Rapid visual acquisition of the next keystroke in <60ms
  • ▸ Clear separation of prompt vs performance feedback
